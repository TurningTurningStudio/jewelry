About the artist

Hello, I’m Daisy.

I’m the maker behind Turning Turning Studio. I create handmade earrings because I can’t keep my hands still, and because I genuinely believe creating things is part of how we stay human.

I design pieces I would wear myself: bold, quirky, colorful. Whenever possible, I thrift my supplies from local reuse stores, both to reduce environmental impact and to challenge myself creatively by working with what already exists.

I also believe the body is a space we get to live in fully and personally. It’s ours to adorn, customize, and celebrate without apology. Making jewelry I would wear myself, and imagining it out in the world on others, is one of the most rewarding parts of what I do.

My work is informed by beadwork traditions across many cultures and histories, including techniques also present in Indigenous beadwork traditions in North America. I am not Native, and I approach these influences with respect for their origins, ongoing living cultures, and the realities of appropriation within craft spaces.

Turning Turning Studio is always open to learning, creating, and crafting.
